Generator and privacy status
----------------------------
The only existing generator is a 5-column fabricated-data toy with hardcoded
age, BMI, glucose, smoking_history, and diabetes. It is not a full CTGAN
implementation for the current EHR and Pima feature schemas.

Opacus 1.6.0 secure mode cannot initialize in the host Python environment
because torchcsprng is missing. Secure noise, secure Poisson sampling, and the
independent Google dp-accounting comparison have not been verified in the
isolated WSL environment. The attempted elevated torchcsprng installation and
subsequent read-only probe were rejected. Training was not started, and there
is no epsilon, DCR, KS, categorical comparison, or synthetic dataset to report.

No ordinary CTGAN or non-private fallback was used. The prior baseline epsilon
is not reused as a guarantee: its sampling generator was seeded. The EHR and
Pima preprocessing reports also document train-derived median/mode imputation
and fitted transforms. A downstream generator accountant would not account
for those prior data-dependent preprocessing outputs, so an end-to-end privacy
claim would require addressing them.

No real-data preprocessing, validation, test files, or baseline files were
modified. No DP-CTGAN training, KAN, federated learning, or downstream work
was started.
